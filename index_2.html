The Season of Giving

Every mile helps a child

A portion of all money raised is donated to the Toys for Tots Foundation, along with every toy collected on race day. Toys for Tots delivers new toys to children in need at Christmas. Bring an unwrapped toy to check-in and help us fill the bins.

Good to know

Can I walk the course?

Yes. Walkers are welcome. Wear shoes with good grip, because the trail surface can be uneven, wet or covered in leaves in December.

Can kids run?

Yes. Runners in high school and under compete in their own category. A parent or guardian must complete the registration and accept the waiver for anyone under 18.

What kind of toy should I bring?

A new, unwrapped toy. Toy donations are optional and are not required to race.
